Privacy Policy for [Phrase Looper]

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

[ocybin-solo] built the [Phrase Looper] app as a local audio recording and editing tool. This Privacy Policy explains how your information and device permissions are handled when you use the app.

1. Information Collection and Use

[Phrase Looper] does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage analytics. All audio recording, track manipulation, and file saving occur locally on your device. We do not operate external servers to receive or process your audio files or personal information.

2. Device Permissions

To provide its core features, the app requests the following permissions on your device:

  • Microphone (Audio Recording): Used strictly to capture live audio when you actively initiate a recording inside the app. Audio is processed locally and is never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • Device Storage / Media Files: Used solely to save your exported audio tracks to your local device storage so you can access, manage, or manually share them using other apps on your phone.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties. If you choose to share a saved audio file with others, that action is initiated manually by you through your device's file system or third-party sharing apps, which are governed by their own respective privacy policies.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or store your data on external servers, we retain no copies of your recordings. Your saved audio tracks remain strictly on your device until you choose to delete them through your device's file manager or by uninstalling the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if new features are added to the app. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
